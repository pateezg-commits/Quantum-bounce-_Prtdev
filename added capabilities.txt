
Quantum bounce 4ds
🗝️ New Mechanics:
3D Mouse Control: * X-Axis: Move the mouse left/right.

Y-Axis: Move the mouse up/down.

Z-Axis (In/Out): The paddle now vibrates back and forth in depth based on the stability level. You have to time your 3D position to match the balls.

Paddle Ghosting:

Real Paddle (Solid White): Can only bounce the Cyan (Real) ball.

Ghost Paddle (Transparent Purple): Can only bounce the Magenta (Ghost) ball.

If you try to catch the wrong ball, it will simply phase through the paddle.

Manual State Flip: Press SPACE to manually toggle the paddle between Real and Ghost states.

Entangled Identity: When the balls hit the ceiling and swap, the paddle has a 50% chance to flip its state too, forcing you to react instantly.
